Explain whether you seek a copy, correction, restriction or erasure, and whether
your node participation should stop. Include a safe device identifier or key
prefix if known. Do not send a full key, token or password. Proportionate
verification of your connection to the device may be needed.

## Handling a request

The operator handles requests manually. Where GDPR applies, a response is due
within its applicable period, usually one month. An allowed extension must be
communicated with a reason. If specific records must be retained on a lawful
basis, the response should identify the basis and retention period.

## Actions that are not complete erasure

Uninstalling stops local application operation but does not automatically erase
server history. Deleting a key in the administrator panel revokes access while
retaining a technical record. Clearing the list can merely hide a user.

A universal automated erasure procedure for related records and backup rotation
is not implemented. This prepared page does not promise a working automated
deletion service. See the [privacy policy](https://handshakevpn.tech/privacy)
for details and current limitations.
